Executive Sales & Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track organizational revenue attainment, profit performance, regional sales distribution, target gaps, and channel breakdowns across multi-tiered product lines.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

![Executive Sales & Performance Dashboard]  https://github.com/suneel-kumar88/Executive-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-/blob/9e865ac234e93dd452e25e6431422e62e81056e2/Executive%20Sales%20Dashboard.png


This dashboard gives sales leaders, commercial directors, and business executives visibility into revenue trajectories, target realization, distribution channel efficiency, and monthly profitability margins.

---

## 🚀 Key KPI Indicators

* **Total Sales:** 17M
* **Total Profit:** 5.09M
* **Profit Margin:** 29% (0.29)
* **Unique Customers:** 84 accounts
* **Total Orders:** 208 orders
* **Target vs Total Sales:** Actual sales of 17M against a 27M target benchmark on an 810 to 35M scale

---

## 📊 Visual Breakdown & Insights

### 1. Channel Performance & Contribution
* **Total Sales by Channel (Donut Chart):** Direct sales lead with 43%, Dealer networks contribute 35%, and Online accounts for 22% of total 17M sales.
* **Total Sales by Channel (Volume Analysis):** Direct generates 7.5M, Dealer produces 6.0M, and Online delivers 3.8.

### 2. Regional Sales & Target Attainment
* **Sales Share by Region:** North accounts for 40%, followed by West (22%), South (19%), and East (19%).
* **Target vs. Actual Sales by Region:**
  * **North:** 7.0M Actual vs. 12.8M Target
  * **West:** 3.8M Actual vs. 7.3M Target
  * **South:** 3.3M Actual vs. 8.7M Target
  * **East:** 3.2M Actual vs. 8.6M Target

### 3. Monthly Trends (January – August 2026)
* **Total Sales by Month:** Sales remain steady between 1.8M–2.2M during Q1, experience an expansion in May (3.9M), dip in June (1.1M), recover in July (2.9M), and finish August at 1.4M.
* **Total Profit by Month:** Mirrors the revenue profile with peak earnings in May (1.02M) and July (0.86M), maintaining an average between 0.35M and 0.68M in other months.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters

* **Text Search Inputs:** Direct interactive lookups for `OrderID` and `Customer`.
* **Time Hierarchy Slicers:** Date filtering across `Year, Quarter, Month, Day`, as well as dedicated `Month` and `Quarter` selectors.
* **Dimension Slicers:** Dynamic filtering across `Region`, `Channel`, `Product`, `Sales person`, `Payment Mode`, and `SubCategory`.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Parameter:** for 2026 data
